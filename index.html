Where Manipur's workshops meet campus expertise.

Search registered MSMEs by what they make or do, or find faculty whose specialisation can help them.

How it works

If you teach or research

Type what you need, such as wool, bamboo, a chemical or a food product. Forge searches what each registered business actually does and shows which districts have them.

If you run a business

Type a skill or a problem, such as machine learning, IoT, marketing or finance. You get faculty with that specialisation, with their official email and profile.

Suggested matches

Open Who can help on any business to see faculty whose expertise fits its sector. These are suggestions based on sector and specialisation, not endorsements.

Business details come from public Udyam MSME registrations. Faculty details come from official institutional directories. Forge shows only what those sources publish.
